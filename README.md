# JavaFinalProject
## Overcooked Inspired Restaurant Game
The player in this game is a FAME student that must run the cafe one day at ENSEA. The objective is to achieve the highest score possible by delivering the correct orders to the students and faculty as quickly as possible.

### Specifications:
- 2 "machine" stations, drinks and food
- 1 current plate station
- 1 type of customer
  - customer orders are randomly generated
  - customer ready to order times are randomly generated
- player must take orders from customers, complete the orders, and deliver the orders to the customers
  - scoring is based on the accuracy the order was completed
  - an accurate order adds 500 points to the total score, and an inaccurate order is 0 points

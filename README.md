# JavaFinalProject
## Overcooked Inspired Restaurant Game
The player in this game is a FAME student that must run the KFET one day at ENSEA. There are 3 levels of the game: one for the pause in the morning, one for lunch, and one for the pause in the afternoon. Each level will increase in difficulty. The objective is to achieve the highest score possible by delivering the correct orders to the students and faculty as quickly as possible.

### Specifications:
- 3 levels with increasing difficulty
- 2 types of customers: students and faculty
  - students judge more leniently (easier to get higher points) while faculty judge more hashly (harder to get higher points)
- player must take orders from customers, complete the orders, and deliver the orders to the customers
  - scoring will be based on how quick and accurate the order was completed
- all scores will be given out of 20
- player wins if the average score at the end of the day is 15/20 or higher
